# Hook system

For Spanish content, also mine `hook-library.md` in this folder: 1000 categorized Spanish hooks with example links, distilled from Javi's "1000 HOOKS VIRALES" doc. Pick category by format family, rewrite placeholders with real specifics, then apply the rejection tests below before keeping any hook.


A hook is the first credible reason to continue. It must set a contract the content fulfills.

## Hook families

1. Specific utility
   - "3 checks before you [high-stakes action]."
   - Strong when the viewer already has intent.

2. Costly mistake
   - "The habit that kept [specific outcome] stuck for six months."
   - Show the consequence quickly; avoid fear inflation.

3. Counterexpectation
   - "I stopped using [popular method]. This worked better for [condition]."
   - Name the boundary; do not universalize one experience.

4. Identity recognition
   - "If you're the person who [precise behavior], this will feel familiar."
   - Use one concrete behavior, not a demographic label alone.

5. Unfinished action
   - Start cutting, assembling, testing, revealing, transforming, or comparing something.
   - The action must finish or materially advance.

6. Result first
   - Show the finished output, then explain the mechanism.
   - Best for visual craft, technical work, transformation, or proof.

7. Earned authority
   - "After [specific volume/time/result], these are the two patterns I trust."
   - Credentials must be true and relevant.

8. Story turn
   - "I thought [belief] until [event]."
   - The event should appear early, not after biography.

9. Tension question
   - A question with real uncertainty: "Why did the worse-looking version outperform the polished one?"
   - Avoid questions whose obvious answer is "yes."

10. Social contrast
   - "What beginners optimize vs what experienced [role] checks first."
   - Avoid humiliating beginners.

## Hook construction

Combine:
- Viewer: who/what state.
- Tension: gap, risk, desire, contradiction, unfinished motion.
- Promise: what becomes clearer or changes.
- Constraint: time, number, condition, evidence, or format.

Example formula: [viewer state] + [unexpected tension] + [bounded promise].

## On-screen legibility

- One thought per screen.
- Keep the first frame readable without audio.
- Use concrete nouns and verbs.
- Avoid stacked qualifiers.
- Time text to actual reading speed. Read it aloud and test at normal playback.
- Preserve safe areas for platform controls and captions.

## Hook rejection tests

Reject when:
- The body could follow almost any hook.
- The claim is stronger than the evidence.
- The viewer must know hidden context.
- The novelty is only an adjective: "crazy", "insane", "game-changing."
- It delays the subject to manufacture suspense.
- It creates anxiety without helping resolve it.
- It copies distinctive source wording.

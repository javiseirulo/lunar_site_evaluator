# Format taxonomy

## Advice

Promise: "You will leave with a usable model, list, method, or correction."

Subtypes:
- Numbered checklist: count plus concrete domain.
- Problem -> named solution pairs.
- Expert candor: advice the creator would give without softening it.
- Unexpected method: strange or counterintuitive advice with personal proof.
- Saveable reference: large list, cheat sheet, template, comparison.
- Role/process explainer: what a practitioner actually does.
- Demonstrated tip: instruction synchronized with visible execution.

Best signals: saves, rewatches, follows from qualified viewers.
Failure modes: obvious advice, unsupported claims, oversized captions, generic expertise.

## Opinion

Promise: "You will hear a clear interpretation that helps you decide, notice, or disagree."

Subtypes:
- Unpopular opinion.
- Myth/misconception correction.
- Ranking or tiering with reasons.
- Reaction/duet/stitch-style commentary.
- "Stop doing X" boundary.
- Two-sided tradeoff or dilemma.
- Cultural observation anchored in a specific example.

Best signals: qualified comments, shares to debate, follows for worldview.
Failure modes: rage bait, no reasoning, attacking a person instead of an idea, consensus disguised as contrarianism.

## Relatable

Promise: "You will recognize yourself or someone close to you."

Subtypes:
- POV micro-scene.
- Specific-audience truth.
- Expectation versus reality.
- Internal monologue.
- Friendship/family/work archetype.
- Identity countdown or progression.
- Validating list of what the audience really wants.

Best signals: shares, tags, replays, affinity comments.
Failure modes: universal platitudes, stereotype harm, overexplaining the joke, no concrete detail.

## Easy-to-use

Promise: "A simple visual/text device will deliver one clear idea with low production burden."

Subtypes:
- Text over one B-roll loop.
- Countdown or rating sequence.
- Before/after labels.
- One quote direct to camera.
- Side-by-side check/cross.
- Trend audio with niche-specific text.
- Read-the-caption bridge.

Best signals: production consistency, fast testing, reach when the text is strong.
Failure modes: text too dense for screen time, irrelevant B-roll, dependence on a trend with no audience fit, hollow caption bridge.

## Storytelling

Promise: "A sequence of change, conflict, or discovery will produce a meaningful payoff."

Subtypes:
- Origin/transformation.
- Failure -> pivot -> result.
- Confession or hidden context.
- Case study with causality.
- Suspense/reveal.
- Lesson earned through cost.
- Documentary/chronological account.

Best signals: completion, rewatch, saves, deeper follows.
Failure modes: chronological throat-clearing, vague stakes, too many characters, moral before experience, no causal turn.

## Cross-cutting mechanisms

These are mechanisms, not full formats:
- Curiosity gap.
- Open loop.
- Specific number.
- Contradiction.
- Identity signal.
- Status or expertise.
- Visual unfinished action.
- Pattern break.
- Escalation.
- Proof.
- Emotional validation.
- Surprise reframe.
- Retrieval value.
- Participation prompt.

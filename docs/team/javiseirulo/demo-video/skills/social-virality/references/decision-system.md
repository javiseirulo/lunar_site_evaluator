# Decision system

## 1. Start with the intended behavior

| Desired outcome | Best starting families | Core value | Common mistake |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reach among cold viewers | Relatable, opinion, easy-to-use | Fast recognition or tension | Explaining before earning attention |
| Saves | Advice, checklists, templates, named methods | Reusable utility | Too many shallow points or no retrieval cue |
| Shares/tags | Relatable, identity, validation | "This is us/you" social currency | Generic statements that fit everyone |
| Comments | Opinion, dilemma, ranking, confession | A genuine position or choice | Empty "what do you think?" bait |
| Authority | Advice, role explainer, teardown, evidence-led opinion | Specific expertise and proof | Claiming certainty without evidence |
| Community bonding | Relatable, story, vulnerability, insider shorthand | Seen-ness and belonging | Performing vulnerability without stakes |
| Conversion | Demonstration, case story, objection reversal, problem-solution | Credible proof and fit | Pitching before value or hiding conditions |

## 2. Diagnose the source material

Choose the strongest asset already present:

- A sharp belief: use opinion or myth correction.
- A specific transformation with evidence: use story or demonstration.
- A repeatable method: use advice, checklist, or named technique.
- A common private experience: use relatable/POV/identity.
- Visually satisfying action: use easy-to-use text-over-action or process payoff.
- A surprising artifact, comparison, number, or mistake: use curiosity plus proof.
- A nuanced emotional journey: use storytelling, not a compressed list.

If no asset exists, research or gather one. Editing cannot manufacture substance.

## 3. Select emotional temperature

- High arousal: surprise, outrage, urgency, awe. Good for reach, risky for trust.
- Medium arousal: curiosity, recognition, aspiration. Broadly reusable.
- Low arousal: calm validation, intimacy, reflection. Needs precise language and usually shorter length.

Match delivery, cuts, typography, and music. A calm message with frantic editing is incoherent.

## 4. Complexity gate

- One insight, one reveal, or one joke: 5-15 seconds.
- Three linked points: 15-35 seconds.
- Demonstration or short explanation: 20-45 seconds.
- Story with setup, turn, and meaning: 35-90 seconds.
- If more than one conceptual turn is necessary, use a series or long-form.

## 5. Evidence and risk gate

For each claim label it:

- Personal observation.
- Demonstration visible in the video.
- Verifiable fact with a source.
- Interpretation/opinion.
- Hypothesis.

High-risk topics need sources and careful wording. Never inherit factual certainty from a viral example.

## 6. Format-selection rules

Use Advice when the viewer can act later and saving is rational.
Use Relatable when recognition itself is the payoff.
Use Opinion when a defensible stance creates useful tension.
Use Easy-to-use when production simplicity and repeatability matter most.
Use Storytelling when change over time is the value and the final meaning depends on earlier context.

Do not mix all five. One dominant contract makes a short legible.

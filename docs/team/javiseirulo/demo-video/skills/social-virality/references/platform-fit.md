# Platform fit

Platform behavior changes. Treat these as packaging heuristics, then check current product rules and analytics.

## TikTok

- Native-feeling delivery, fast context, strong comment culture, trend literacy.
- Test looser performance and direct language before adding polish.
- Search intent matters for educational content: say and write the topic clearly.
- Replies, stitches, and recurring formats can turn comments into content.

## Instagram Reels

- Strong visual identity and shareability in DMs often matter.
- Carousels/captions can complement a Reel when the material is save-heavy.
- Covers and profile-grid coherence affect second-order discovery.
- Repost/share language should feel socially useful, not manipulative.

## YouTube Shorts

- Clear topic and durable viewer value can help beyond a trend window.
- Series, channel expertise, and connection to long-form are useful.
- A satisfying standalone arc matters; avoid assuming prior episode context.
- Titles still carry topic information even when first-frame packaging does the immediate hook work.

## Cross-posting

Do not only remove a watermark. Adapt:
- Caption syntax and length.
- CTA behavior.
- Music rights/availability.
- Cover/title.
- Search phrasing.
- Safe areas.
- Comment/reply affordances.
- Link or conversion path.

Keep the core mechanism stable so the test remains interpretable.
